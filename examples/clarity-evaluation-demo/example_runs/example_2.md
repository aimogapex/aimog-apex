## **User Request**
“Give me a concise, non‑technical explanation of cloud computing suitable for an executive audience.”

---

## **Draft Response (Generated)**
**1. Core idea**  
Cloud computing means using computing power, storage, and services hosted in large data centers rather than maintaining those resources internally.

**2. Executive‑level analogy**  
It is similar to shifting from owning physical infrastructure to consuming it as a utility — predictable, scalable, and maintained by specialists.

**3. Essential components**  
- compute (processing)  
- storage (data)  
- networking (connectivity)  
- managed services (higher‑level capabilities)  

**4. Operational framing**  
Instead of provisioning servers, teams consume capacity on demand, paying only for what they use and scaling without hardware cycles.

**5. Next step**  
Identify one internal workload that could benefit from elasticity or reduced operational overhead.
